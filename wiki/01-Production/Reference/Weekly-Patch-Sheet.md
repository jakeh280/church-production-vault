# Weekly patch sheet

> [!info] Live document. This page is a pointer, not a copy
> The authoritative version lives in the shared spreadsheet and changes every week. **Nothing on this page is a frozen copy of its contents.** Pages with `live_doc: true` are excluded from [[Verification-Dashboard]], because there is nothing here to go stale.

## What it is

The input list for the coming weekend, who's playing, what's plugged in where. Rebuilt weekly by whoever is on audio.

## Where it lives

The team's shared drive, in the production folder. Access is through the team account; credentials live in the password manager, not here.

## The durable fact

The part that doesn't change week to week is the **naming convention**: `<position>-<source>`, matching the console labels described in [[Audio-System]]. Every weekly sheet follows it, which is why a stage plot from six months ago is still readable.
