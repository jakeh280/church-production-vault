# Team Structure

Roles and reporting lines only.

> [!warning] What does not go on this page
> No performance notes, no attendance records, no personal contact details, no compensation, no commentary about individuals. This page answers "who covers what" and nothing else. See `CLAUDE.md` → What Never Goes in This Vault.

## Reporting lines

- **Production lead**: owns the booth, the gear, and the volunteer team. Reports to the creative or worship lead.
- **Audio**: one paid or lead volunteer, plus rotating volunteers
- **Video / stream**: rotating volunteers
- **Presentation / lyrics**: rotating volunteers

## Adjacent roles

People outside the production team who touch production gear, and what they touch:

- **Worship lead**: stage plot, in-ear mixes, rehearsal schedule
- **Facilities**: house lighting, HVAC, room access

## Volunteers

Rotation shape only, how many serve per weekend and on what cycle. Names and schedules live in whatever system does scheduling, never here.
